Delete Directly Inside the App (Instant)

The fastest way to remove your data is directly inside the SquadPulse application. Follow these instructions:

  1. Open SquadPulse and navigate to your User Profile screen.
  2. Tap the Profile Edit Icon located near your current profile card.

    [ Screenshot Placeholder: Profile screen highlighting the Edit Icon ]

  3. Scroll completely to the bottom of the Edit Profile screen and tap the Delete Account option. Confirm the prompt to instantly delete your profile and historical records.

    [ Screenshot Placeholder: Bottom of Edit Profile screen showing the Deletion button ]

Request Deletion Via Web

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot log in, use the form below to submit a deletion request. Our team will process your request within 7 business days.

What data will be removed?
Proceeding will permanently delete your authentication login credentials, nickname registry, profile statistics (wins, streaks, levels), coin balances, and any historical match data associated with your Unique User ID (`uid`).
